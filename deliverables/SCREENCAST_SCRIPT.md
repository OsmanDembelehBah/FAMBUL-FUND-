# 🎬 Fambul Fund 90-Second Screencast Script

**Target Duration:** Exactly 90 seconds (1 minute, 30 seconds)  
**Tone:** Confident, clear, professional West African fintech pitch  
**Visual Style:** Live browser recording cycling between USSD feature phone, Farmer PWA, Buyer Console, and Lender Dashboard.

---

### [00:00 - 00:15] The Hook & The Problem
- **Visual:** Open on the **Landing Page**. Highlight the statistic: *"70%+ of Sierra Leoneans save in Osusu groups, yet lack bank credit."*
- **Voiceover (VO):**  
  > *"In Sierra Leone, rural smallholders are among the most disciplined savers in the world, rotating millions of Leones weekly in village Osusus. Yet because these transactions happen on paper notebooks or in memory, commercial banks treat them as unbankable. Meet Fambul Fund: the digital credit and repayment infrastructure turning informal Osusu groups into bankable agricultural units."*

---

### [00:15 - 00:35] Farmer USSD & Spoken Krio Voice Ledger
- **Visual:** Switch to **USSD Phone Simulator**. Dial `*714#`, navigate to `4. Record` and log 10 bags of rice. Then switch to **Farmer PWA** and tap the microphone. Show Krio voice note parsing: *"A sel 5 bag rays tide na Bo maket fɔ 250 Leone"*.
- **VO:**  
  > *"Fambul Fund works on any phone. On a basic feature phone, farmers dial star-7-1-4-hash to check balances or record sales in seconds under strict 160-character screens. On smartphones, our offline-first PWA listens in spoken Krio or English, transcribing harvest notes with farmer confirmation before anything touches the ledger."*

---

### [00:35 - 00:55] Transparent Group Scoring & Pot Advance
- **Visual:** Click the credit badge `88.8 (Tier A)`. Open the **Group Credit Score Explainer** modal showing the 5 transparent weighted pillars (35% consistency, 25% repayment, 20% sales). Then show **G1 Pot Advance** card.
- **VO:**  
  > *"Every transaction feeds a deterministic 5-pillar credit scoring model, weighting Osusu discipline at 35% and certified buyer sales at 20%. Prime groups unlock institutional borrowing capped within Sierra Leone's statutory 15% ceiling—and farmers can request an early Pot Advance right when the lean season hits."*

---

### [00:55 - 01:15] Buyer Console: 4-Way Repayment-at-Source
- **Visual:** Switch to **Buyer Payout Console**. Show the 5-row table with Gross, Loan Repay, Osusu, Fee, Net. Click **Execute 4-Way Split Payout**. Watch progress bar complete, turning lines green and issuing digital vouchers.
- **VO:**  
  > *"Here is the breakthrough: Repayment-at-source. When certified aggregators purchase the harvest, our payout engine executes a 4-way split: deducting the input loan, reserving the Osusu pot share, and taking a 1% fee, before instantly disbursing net mobile money via Orange or Afrimoney."*

---

### [01:15 - 01:30] Closed-Loop Settlement & Conclusion
- **Visual:** Switch to **Lender Dashboard**. Point out the loan balance settling to zero live, PAR30 at 2.1%, and the SMS confirmation popping up in the phone inbox.
- **VO:**  
  > *"Instantly, the lender's dashboard updates in real time. Zero collection overhead, zero manual chasing, and rural farmers build permanent financial identities. Fambul Fund: Digitize Osusu, protect the harvest, and bank the nation. Thank you."*
